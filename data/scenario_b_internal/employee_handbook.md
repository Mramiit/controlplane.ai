# Employee Handbook: Benefits and Policies 2026

## 1. Paid Time Off (PTO)
*   **Standard Accrual:** Full-time employees accrue 15 days of Paid Time Off (PTO) per calendar year.
*   **Rollover:** Employees may roll over a maximum of 5 unused PTO days into the following calendar year. Any additional unused days will be forfeited.
*   **Sick Leave:** Employees receive 7 days of paid sick leave annually, separate from standard PTO.

## 2. Parental Leave
*   **Primary Caregiver:** Eligible for 12 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
*   **Secondary Caregiver:** Eligible for 4 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
*   Eligibility begins after 6 months of continuous full-time employment.

## 3. Remote Work and Equipment
*   **Home Office Stipend:** New hires receive a one-time $300 stipend to set up their home office equipment.
*   **Internet Reimbursement:** The company reimburses up to $50 per month for home internet expenses.
*   **Hardware:** Employees are provided a standard company laptop. Upgrades require director-level approval.

## 4. Health and Wellness
*   **Wellness Stipend:** Employees receive an annual wellness stipend of $500, which can be used for gym memberships, mental health apps, or fitness equipment.
*   The wellness stipend is paid out in the first paycheck of February and is subject to standard income tax.
