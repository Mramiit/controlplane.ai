# Horizon Electronics - Customer Support FAQ

1. Returns and Refunds
- Returns are accepted within 30 days of the delivery date.
- Items must be in original packaging and condition to qualify for a full refund.
- Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after the warehouse receives the return.
- Clearance items and digital gift cards are non-refundable.

2. Shipping and Delivery
- Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days and is free for orders over $50.
- Expedited shipping takes 1-2 business days and costs a flat rate of $15.
- We currently do not ship internationally. Deliveries are restricted to the contiguous United States.
- If a package is marked as delivered but missing, customers must wait 24 hours before filing a claim.

3. Warranty
- All laptops and smartphones come with a standard 1-year manufacturer warranty covering hardware defects.
- Accidental damage (e.g., water damage, screen cracks) is not covered under the standard warranty.
- Extended protection plans must be purchased within 14 days of the original device purchase.

4. Account and Security
- Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and contain a special character.
- Customer support will never ask for your full credit card number or password over chat.
